Vision2Slope is an open framework that estimates urban road slopes from panoramic street view imagery using computer vision.
It projects panoramas into road-aligned views, corrects camera-induced distortion, and derives point- and segment-level slope and relief.

The method is described in our paper in the [*International Journal of Geographical Information Science*](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2026.2656269), and the code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/CubicsYang/Vision2Slope).
